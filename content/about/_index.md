Hello, visitor.

I am a programmer (just like many) from the World **Wide** Web, as well as a cybersecurity, privacy, and FOSS enthusasist.
I like things fast, open source, secure, and private.
I currently use [NixOS](https://cachyos.org/) ([config available here](https://git.csd4ni3l.hu/csd4ni3l/nixos-config)) because of it's insane customizability and reproducibility, and I think I've made quite a good hardened but performant config for myself.
I self-host lot of my stuff on [Proxmox VE](https://www.proxmox.com/en/products/proxmox-virtual-environment/overview) on a mini PC, and try to use FOSS software when i can.
I also self-host Pihole for DHCP & DNS on my network.
